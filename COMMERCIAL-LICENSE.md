# Commercial licence

kb-engine is published under AGPL-3.0-or-later. That licence is a good fit for
personal use, for self-hosting, and for organisations that are willing to
publish their modifications. It is a poor fit for two situations:

- you want to embed the engine in a product you distribute under closed terms;
- you run a modified engine as a service and cannot publish the modified source.

For those cases a separate commercial licence is available, granting the same
code under terms without the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations.

## What the commercial licence covers

- The engine's source code in this repository.
- Use in closed-source products, whether distributed or offered as a service.
- Modification without the obligation to publish the modified source.

It does not cover third-party dependencies, which keep their own licences (see
`go.mod` and `frontend/package-lock.json`).

## What it does not change

The AGPL version stays available and maintained. A commercial licence is an
alternative set of terms for the same code, not a different or better build.

Write to **daniilvdovin4@gmail.com** with a short description of how the engine

## Which versions this applies to

Releases up to and including `v0.3.0` were published under MIT and remain
available under MIT to anyone who obtained them — an MIT grant cannot be
withdrawn after the fact. The dual-licence arrangement described here applies
from `v0.4.0` onward.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Because the project offers
a commercial licence alongside it, a contributor licence agreement will be
required before non-trivial contributions can be merged; none has been needed
so far, as every commit to date is the author's own.
